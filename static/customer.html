          Usage alerts and limits
          

          Alerts appear at 80%, 95%, and 100% of your included minutes. We never bill additional minutes without your approval.
          

          Checking whether automatic inbound-call limits are active…
          

          Each AI call has a hard four-minute maximum. When the monthly minutes are used, new AI calls are declined until renewal or a plan upgrade; an existing call may finish up to its four-minute maximum. There is no daily call limit or automatic overage charge.
