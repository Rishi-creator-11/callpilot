Common questions

Before your first call

Can I keep my business number?

You can keep your public number and forward calls to a connected CallPilot number, subject to your carrier’s forwarding options.

Can I review imported information first?

Imported pages arrive as drafts. Workspace owners and admins can edit and approve the text in the Knowledge base.

What if a caller asks for a price?

You can choose to allow exact owner-approved prices. CallPilot can collect the request for a callback when a verified price is unavailable and lead capture is enabled.

How are recordings and transcripts handled?

Authorized workspace members can review available recordings and transcripts. See the privacy and recording notice for details.
