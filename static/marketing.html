Common questions

Before your first call.

Can I keep my business number?

You can keep your public number and forward calls to a connected CallPilot number, subject to your carrier’s options. See the setup steps.

Can I review imported information first?

Yes. Website and PDF imports arrive as drafts. Owners and admins can edit and approve them in the knowledge base.

What if a caller asks for a price?

You can enable exact owner-approved prices. When a verified price is unavailable, an enabled callback workflow can collect a request for your team.

Can I test an answer before connecting a number?

Yes. Open Test assistant and ask a question to see which approved information CallPilot retrieves.

Does a pending order request charge the caller?

No. Your team reviews the request and handles payment and fulfillment separately.

Who can access my call history?

Authorized workspace members can review available recordings and transcripts. See the privacy and recording notice.
