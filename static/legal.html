Plans, terms, privacy and recording notice

Draft for review. This page states how CallPilot works today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and it does not replace formal Terms of Service or a Privacy Policy. Items marked “to be confirmed” are decisions the CallPilot owner has not finalized.

Plans and limits

PlanPriceIncluded AI call minutes per month
Starter$49 / month100
Growth$99 / month300
Business$179 / month600
  • A one-time $100 professional setup fee is charged per business with the first successful subscription payment. It is not charged again on renewals or after a cancelled plan is restarted.
  • Every AI call has a hard maximum of four minutes (240 seconds).
  • Minutes are measured from actual completed inbound AI calls reported by the voice provider, not estimates. Estimated call counts assume two-minute calls.
  • When your monthly minutes are used, new AI calls are declined until the next billing cycle or an upgrade. A call already in progress is not cut off for that reason, so one final call can run past the remaining minutes by up to four minutes.
  • No automatic overage charges. There is no daily call limit.
  • If you turn on forwarding to a human number, callers can be forwarded when AI minutes run out. Your phone carrier may charge for forwarded calls. Forwarding is off unless you enable it.
  • You get dashboard alerts at 80%, 95% and 100% of your included minutes.

Billing, cancellation and refunds

  • Payments, invoices and cancellation are handled by Stripe through the billing portal in your dashboard.
  • Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Cancellation timing, proration and any refund of the setup fee or subscription fees: to be confirmed by CallPilot before accepting live payments.
  • If a payment fails, the subscription is marked past due and new AI calls may be declined until payment is resolved.

Call recording and AI disclosure

  • The AI receptionist tells callers at the start of the call that it is an AI assistant.
  • Calls can be recorded and transcribed so the business can review them. Laws about recording phone calls differ by place; some require callers to be told or to consent. Each business is responsible for any additional recording notice or consent its callers need. Wording for caller consent: to be confirmed.
  • Recordings and transcripts are shown only to signed-in members of the business they belong to.
  • Retention period and deletion process for recordings: to be confirmed.

Privacy

  • CallPilot stores your account details, business information, approved knowledge, call records, transcripts, summaries and lead notes.
  • Service providers process this data to run the service: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vapi (phone calls and AI voice), Stripe (payments) and Google (calendar, only if you connect it).
  • Each business’s data is kept separate from every other business’s data.
  • CallPilot does not sell personal data. Contact and data-request process: to be confirmed.

Acceptable use and accuracy

  • The AI only gives answers from knowledge the business has approved. It does not quote prices or estimates, and it offers human follow-up when it does not know.
  • CallPilot does not guarantee that any caller will become a customer, and lead labels are suggestions, not verification of intent or ability to pay.
